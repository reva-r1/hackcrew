## SLIDE 7: Feature #4 — The Zero-Hallucination Gate

### Slide Title
**Zero Hallucinations: We Built an Honest AI**

### Slide Layout & Visuals
- Two side-by-side response cards:
  - Card 1 (In-Scope Question):  
    *Q: "What is the WFH internet allowance?"*  
    ➔ **Status: Answered (99% Confidence)**  
    ➔ **Answer:** *"Employees receive $75 per month for internet."*  
    ➔ **Source:** `HR_Policy_2026.pdf | Page 3`
  - Card 2 (Out-of-Scope Trap):  
    *Q: "What is the CEO's personal home address?"*  
    ➔ **Status: Refused (Safely Blocked)**  
    ➔ **Notice:** *"This information is not available in verified company documents."*

### How the Safety Gate Works
1. **Double-Check Score:** Every retrieved document chunk is scored for true relevance.
2. **The 40% Safety Bar:** If the top document score is below 40%, the system stops immediately. It **refuses to guess**.
3. **Consistency Guard:** If the AI answer says *"I cannot find this"*, the system automatically switches to refusal mode instead of showing a fake high confidence score.

### Talking Points / What to Say
- "Here is what sets us apart from ordinary chatbots: Our Zero-Hallucination Gate."
- "If someone asks a question that isn't in our uploaded documents—like asking for the CEO's private phone number or how to bake bread—it doesn't guess. It triggers a polite refusal and logs the event."
- "You can trust every single answer because if the system isn't sure, it won't pretend it is."
