# SHIPPING COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the policies and operational guidelines for the shipping company to ensure safe, reliable, efficient, and transparent transportation of goods.

The policies apply to employees, delivery personnel, warehouse staff, customers, logistics partners, and other parties involved in the shipping process.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers:

* Shipment booking and processing
* Package handling and transportation
* Warehouse operations
* Delivery operations
* Shipment tracking
* Customer communication
* Damaged, lost, or delayed shipments
* Restricted and prohibited items
* Data security and privacy
* Payment and refund procedures
* Complaints and dispute resolution
* Safety and compliance

## 3. Shipment Acceptance Policy

All shipments must contain accurate information about:

* Sender details
* Receiver details
* Pickup and delivery addresses
* Package description
* Package weight and dimensions
* Declared value where applicable
* Required documentation

The company reserves the right to inspect or reject shipments that violate company policies or applicable laws.

## 4. Packaging Policy

Customers are responsible for ensuring that shipments are properly packaged.

Packages must be:

* Securely sealed
* Appropriate for the type of goods being transported
* Protected against normal handling conditions
* Clearly labelled with required shipping information

The company may refuse improperly packaged shipments when they present a risk of damage or safety concerns.

## 5. Prohibited Items Policy

The company does not accept items that are prohibited by applicable laws, transportation regulations, or company policy.

Examples may include:

* Illegal goods
* Explosive materials
* Hazardous substances
* Certain flammable materials
* Illegal drugs
* Stolen goods
* Counterfeit products
* Other restricted items identified by applicable regulations

Customers are responsible for declaring shipment contents accurately.

## 6. Shipment Tracking Policy

Each shipment should receive a unique tracking identifier.

Tracking information should be updated at relevant stages, including:

1. Shipment booked
2. Package picked up
3. Package received at facility
4. Package processed
5. Package dispatched
6. Package out for delivery
7. Package delivered

The company will make reasonable efforts to maintain accurate and timely tracking information.

## 7. Delivery Policy

Delivery personnel must make reasonable efforts to deliver shipments to the designated delivery address.

Where delivery cannot be completed, the company may:

* Attempt delivery again
* Contact the recipient
* Hold the package at an authorized facility
* Return the package to the sender

Delivery procedures may vary depending on the shipment type and local requirements.

## 8. Delayed Shipment Policy

A shipment may be delayed because of:

* Severe weather
* Traffic or transportation disruptions
* Operational issues
* Incorrect address information
* Customs or regulatory procedures
* Security incidents
* Unexpected events

The company should provide updated tracking information whenever reasonably possible.

## 9. Lost Shipment Policy

If a shipment cannot be located, the company will initiate an internal investigation.

The investigation may include reviewing:

* Tracking records
* Facility scans
* Transportation records
* Delivery records
* Employee handling records

Customers may be required to provide shipment details and supporting documentation.

## 10. Damaged Shipment Policy

Customers should report damaged shipments as soon as reasonably possible.

The company may investigate the circumstances surrounding the damage and may request:

* Photographs of the package
* Photographs of the contents
* Proof of shipment
* Purchase or declared-value documentation
* Other relevant information

Claims will be evaluated according to the company's terms and applicable regulations.

## 11. Return and Undeliverable Shipment Policy

Shipments may be returned when:

* The recipient refuses delivery
* The address is incorrect or incomplete
* The recipient cannot be reached
* The shipment violates company policy
* Delivery attempts are unsuccessful

Return shipping charges may apply depending on the circumstances.

## 12. Customer Communication Policy

The company should provide customers with clear information regarding:

* Shipment status
* Estimated delivery information
* Delays
* Delivery attempts
* Exceptions
* Claims and complaints

Customer communications should be accurate, professional, and understandable.

## 13. Data Privacy and Security Policy

Customer and shipment information must be protected against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or misuse.

Access to sensitive information should be limited to authorized personnel.

The company should follow applicable data-protection and privacy laws.

## 14. Payment and Refund Policy

Shipping charges must be communicated to customers before or during shipment booking where applicable.

Refunds may be provided according to the company's refund terms.

Additional charges may apply for services such as:

* Additional delivery attempts
* Address correction
* Special handling
* Storage
* Return shipping
* Customs-related services

## 15. Employee Conduct Policy

Employees and delivery personnel must:

* Handle shipments responsibly
* Protect customer information
* Follow safety procedures
* Maintain professional conduct
* Report shipment irregularities
* Avoid unauthorized access or tampering
* Follow company procedures and applicable laws

Fraud, theft, deliberate package damage, or unauthorized disclosure of customer information is prohibited.

## 16. Warehouse Safety Policy

Warehouse personnel must maintain a safe working environment.

The company should implement appropriate procedures for:

* Package stacking
* Heavy packages
* Hazard identification
* Equipment operation
* Fire safety
* Emergency response
* Restricted-access areas

Employees must follow applicable workplace safety requirements.

## 17. Fraud Prevention Policy

The company will take reasonable measures to detect and prevent fraudulent activities.

Potential indicators may include:

* Suspicious shipment information
* False identities
* Unusual shipment patterns
* Fraudulent payment activity
* Repeated claims
* Manipulated tracking information

Suspicious activity may be investigated and reported to the appropriate authorities when required.

## 18. Complaint and Dispute Resolution

Customers may submit complaints regarding:

* Delayed shipments
* Lost shipments
* Damaged packages
* Incorrect charges
* Delivery issues
* Customer service

Complaints should be reviewed fairly and resolved within a reasonable period.

## 19. Regulatory Compliance

The company must comply with applicable transportation, customs, taxation, consumer-protection, data-protection, workplace-safety, and other relevant laws and regulations.

Where regulations conflict with internal company policies, applicable legal requirements take precedence.

## 20. Policy Review

This document should be reviewed periodically to ensure that it remains accurate and aligned with:

* Operational requirements
* Customer needs
* Security standards
* Technology changes
* Industry practices
* Applicable laws and regulations

Changes to the policy must be approved by authorized company management.

## 21. Effective Date

This policy becomes effective from the date approved by the company management and remains applicable until replaced or updated.

**Document Owner:** Shipping Operations Department
**Policy Status:** Active
**Review Frequency:** Periodic
**Approval Authority:** Company Management
